How this guide schedules reviews

Every card carries a memory model borrowed from the FSRS algorithm that Anki uses: a stability (how many days until you'd have a ~90% chance of recalling it) and a difficulty. Rating a card honestly — Again, Hard, Good, Easy — updates both, and a card comes back when its predicted recall drops below 90%. Learn mode (Quizlet-style) starts new cards as multiple-choice, then switches to written recall or step-ordering once they stick.
